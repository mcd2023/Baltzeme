Who we are

A focused European platform for pharmaceutical cooperation.

BaltZeme Invest SIA is a Latvia-based business-development, licensing and portfolio-development platform. We work with pharmaceutical companies seeking thoughtful, well-structured routes into selected markets.

Our work is relationship-led and commercially practical. We bring the right parties together, clarify mutual priorities and help each opportunity progress with purpose.

What we do

Flexible cooperation, shaped around the opportunity.

We evaluate each project individually and establish the most appropriate model with our partners.

01

Licensing & Market Access

Supporting carefully selected pharmaceutical opportunities through commercial evaluation, partner identification and market-access planning.

02

Finished-Product Supply

Connecting established manufacturers with suitable commercial partners for reliable, market-focused supply programmes.

03

Co-development

Bringing complementary capabilities together to structure practical, long-term product development collaborations.

04

Technology Transfer

Facilitating focused discussions around know-how, manufacturing capabilities and appropriate transfer opportunities.
